**Campus Trips** are small-group weekend trips for international students in Korea, organized by Stargate Corporation. The idea is simple: see more of Korea than Seoul, travel with other students, and let someone local handle the planning.

## What we are planning

- **Day trips from Seoul** — palaces and hanok villages, mountain hikes, seaside towns
- **Seasonal trips** — cherry blossoms in spring, autumn leaves in the mountains, winter festivals
- **Weekend trips** — Busan, Gyeongju, Jeonju and other cities that are easy to reach by train

Groups will be small, itineraries will be clear in advance, and guides will speak English.

## Register your interest

Trips are not open for booking yet. If you would like to hear first when the first dates are announced, email us at **[ceo@stargateedu.co.kr](mailto:ceo@stargateedu.co.kr)** with the subject "Campus Trips" and tell us:

1. Your university
2. Which kind of trip you would join
3. The months you are available

We will only use your email to contact you about Campus Trips.
