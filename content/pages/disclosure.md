## Free travel information

Our public itineraries, arrival checklist and articles are free to read. You do not need to give us an email address to use or download the free resources.

The personalized planner uses editorial route ideas and rules applied to the choices you select. It does not call a live AI service or check current prices, route conditions or availability. You can download or print the outline for free. Optional product suggestions explain why a standard book or a separately quoted custom brief may fit your choices.

## Our digital travel book

We offer an original English PDF travel guide, **[Seoul, at your own pace](/books/)**. The product page states the edition, language, format, page count and listed price, and provides a free sample. The full PDF is delivered through our linked store after payment confirmation. It is for personal use and does not include travel services or future edition updates.

Purchases are handled by **[our Stargate Edu store](https://stargateedu.imweb.me/shop_view?idx=10)** on Imweb, operated by 스타게이트. We display the USD equivalent first, followed by the KRW price, using a fixed display rate of US$1 = ₩1,400. The checkout charges Korean won and is currently in Korean. Review the total price, payment method, seller details and cancellation/refund terms there before paying. Confirmed payments unlock the PDF in your order/account; bank-transfer orders require deposit confirmation. Payment or download issues and cancellation/refund requests can be sent to ceo@stargateedu.co.kr with your order number.

## Custom guide enquiries

We accept enquiries for custom travel research and writing. A custom digital guide is an information document. The scope, price, delivery date, revision allowance and cancellation/refund terms must be confirmed in writing before an order or payment.

Custom guide enquiries are separate from the fixed-price PDF store. Preparing an enquiry on this site does not send it or create an order. The visitor chooses whether to send the draft through their own email app.

A digital brief does not include travel reservations, ticketing, accommodation, transport or in-person guiding. Those services are chosen and purchased directly from their providers.

## Provider and affiliate links

The travel essentials page identifies each outbound commercial resource. **Regular links do not earn us affiliate commission.** Any affiliate link activated later will be labeled next to the link. We may receive a commission for a qualifying transaction through a labeled affiliate link.

Listing a provider is not a claim that we have a partnership with it. We do not claim to compare every supplier or offer the lowest price. Review the provider's complete price, availability, conditions and privacy policy before purchasing.

An affiliate relationship does not change the need to check suitability for your own trip. We include practical checks such as device compatibility, arrival time, location, exclusions and cancellation conditions alongside links.

## Advertising and sponsored material

Display advertising is not currently enabled. Any future sponsored editorial placement will be identified clearly. We do not present paid placements as independent reviews or invent visitor testimonials.
