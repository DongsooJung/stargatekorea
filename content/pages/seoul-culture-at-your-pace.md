Culture travel is often better with fewer anchors. Choose the places you are curious about, check their calendars, and give yourself time to read, pause and notice the neighborhood around them.

## One palace is a useful beginning

Use a palace visit as the first part of a Jongno day, then make tea, a meal or nearby lanes your second anchor. Gyeongbokgung, Insadong and Ikseon-dong are starting ideas in our planner, not a fixed schedule. Check the palace opening day and your actual journey before setting the date.

A slower setting keeps the first two core stops. The extra neighborhood is something you can leave for another day without losing the point of the trip.

## Give a museum its own attention

For a separate day, choose a part of the National Museum of Korea that interests you. Check the calendar and whether a particular exhibition has its own ticket or reservation. Leave the next meal simple and nearby rather than attaching another distant landmark.

A temple and an indoor afternoon around Bongeunsa and COEX can be another option. Follow the temple's current visiting rules and respect ceremonies and people using the space.

## Choose time before tickets

- Read one thing you want to understand before each visit.
- Keep a cafe or indoor alternative for weather changes.
- Check photography rules and access at the actual venue.
- Compare any paid experience with what you already plan to do.
- Leave enough time to return to your accommodation comfortably.
