A design-focused Seoul trip needs a good neighborhood base more than a long shopping checklist. Pick a few places you want to explore, put a limit around optional spending, and let the rest of the day stay open.

## Seongsu as one complete day

Use Seongsu-dong as the anchor for shops, cafes and design discoveries. Seoul Forest can be a green pause if the weather and your energy suit it. A pop-up is an optional addition: check its current address, dates and entry arrangements directly rather than building the whole trip around a social post.

## Architecture without an overloaded schedule

DDP can anchor a separate architecture and design day. Choose an exhibition only after checking its program, admission and reservation requirements. Being able to visit a public space does not automatically include entry to every event inside it.

For a different pace, keep a Hongdae and Yeonnam afternoon for independent shops, a cafe and a neighborhood walk. Use the map starting points in the free plan, then check the individual businesses you choose.

## Make the purchases intentional

- Set a shopping allowance separately from food and transport.
- Compare the full price and store terms before buying.
- Confirm your route back with bags before adding a final stop.
- Agree on a meeting point if your group has different interests.
- Leave time for a discovery that was not on the original list.
