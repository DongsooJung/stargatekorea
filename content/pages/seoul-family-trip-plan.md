A good family day has room to change. Start with **two core stops**, one dependable meal idea and a clear way back to your accommodation. A shorter day that everybody enjoys is more useful than a long list of landmarks.

## Start with an indoor anchor

The National Museum of Korea area is one possible anchor for a museum day. Pick a small part of the collection, agree when to stop and plan the meal around your group. Check the museum's own calendar, exhibition terms and access information before choosing the date.

On another day, pair a palace visit with a tea or meal stop around Jongno. Choose your palace after checking its opening day. Leave an extra neighborhood as a possibility rather than a promise to your children.

## Ask the questions that change the route

- Is this a stroller day, or can everyone manage stairs?
- Which station exit works for the exact entrance you will use?
- Where will you stop for toilets, a meal and a longer sit-down break?
- Which ingredients, meal textures or ordering arrangements should you check directly with a restaurant?
- How will you shorten the day if somebody needs to go back early?

Our suggested neighborhoods do not confirm step-free access. Check the current venue and transit details for your own family.

## Keep the spending decision simple

Set a food and transport allowance first. Compare a paid activity only after you have a route that works without it. Ask what the ticket includes, whether an age restriction applies and what happens if your plans change.
