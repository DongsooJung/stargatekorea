A useful weather alternative is already near the day you planned. It should reduce the number of decisions you have to make outside, rather than send you across Seoul to replace every canceled stop.

## Choose an indoor anchor before setting out

A museum, an exhibition you have checked, or an unhurried indoor meal can anchor a shorter day. For a museum option, start with the [National Museum of Korea visitor information](https://www.museum.go.kr/ENG/contents/E0101000000.do). Check its calendar and the terms for the particular exhibition you want. A general museum visit does not automatically include every special exhibition or program.

If the day starts around Jongno, make the nearby meal or tea stop longer rather than treating every lane as mandatory. If it starts around Seongsu or Hongdae, keep the shops you have actually checked and make the outdoor park or long walk optional. Individual businesses choose their own opening days.

## Make the adjustment fit the group

- **Family:** agree on two core stops, a meal pause and a point at which you will head back. Check the specific entrances, lifts and toilets your family needs.
- **Solo:** save the return journey before leaving, then choose an indoor place where you would enjoy spending time alone.
- **Couple:** choose one shared must-do and leave space to change the rest.
- **Friends:** agree on an indoor meeting point and a common spending range before splitting up.

Summer and winter need the same flexible thinking. Shorten exposed walks when the forecast or your group's comfort calls for it. Check current local weather notices before leaving. A seasonal label in a planner is not a forecast for your date.

## Avoid paying twice for the same change

Before buying an alternative activity, check the terms of anything already booked. Compare the complete new cost, arrival arrangements and cancellation terms. Sometimes a slower meal and an earlier return are a better fit than adding another ticket.
