## How these pages are produced

Each weekly page is generated from the official Visit Seoul event listing and checked on a schedule, so the index above always reflects the most recent check rather than a promise about the future. We reproduce the event title, the dates and the original link only, and we do not copy descriptions, images, prices or booking claims. Dates can still change, and events can be cancelled or rescheduled by the organiser, so confirm the details with the venue or organiser before you travel to it. The same caution applies to the seasonal guides linked above, which are written from the general character of each month rather than from live listings.
