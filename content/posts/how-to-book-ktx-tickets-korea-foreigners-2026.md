Planning a trip from Seoul to Busan, or hoping to explore several Korean cities by train? **KTX**, Korea's high-speed railway, is one of the most convenient ways to travel between major destinations. First-time visitors often have questions about which booking website to use, foreign credit cards and whether a KORAIL PASS is better than separate tickets.

This guide focuses on official sources and helps you make the right decision without guessing prices or travel times.

## What is KTX?

KTX stands for Korea Train Express. It is a high-speed train service connecting important Korean cities. Train categories, departure stations and travel times vary by route, so choosing the correct station is just as important as choosing your destination.

For instance, when planning a Seoul-to-Busan trip, check exactly where your selected service departs. Do not assume all trains use the same terminal or that a ticket is valid on a different train.

## Where should foreigners book tickets?

The Korea Tourism Organization identifies **KORAIL's official website and its booking app** as primary reservation channels. Start with [KORAIL](https://www.korail.com/) or the official channel linked from the [VISITKOREA railway guide](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?menuSn=470&vcontsId=140656).

Enter the departure station, destination, travel date and number of passengers. Compare train categories and available departure times. When a fare is displayed, review the refund and change conditions before paying.

Using the official booking channel also makes it easier to verify who issued your ticket. Keep the final booking confirmation accessible during your journey.

## Can you pay with a foreign card?

KORAIL's international-facing services have been improved. In a **May 30, 2025** report, the Korea Tourism Organization described changes to the foreign traveler booking experience, including multilingual seat selection.

However, do not assume every foreign debit card, credit card or mobile wallet will be accepted. Payment compatibility can depend on the specific booking channel and card issuer. Confirm the options displayed at checkout.

If an online payment fails, check official alternatives such as a station ticket counter. Do not transfer money to an unknown intermediary promising an unofficial reservation.

## When should you book?

The Korea Tourism Organization's train guide describes ordinary ticket sales opening around a month before departure. Special periods, including major holidays, may follow a different booking schedule.

If you have a time-sensitive connection, look for tickets as soon as your travel date becomes eligible. Verify the train's departure date and time in **Korea Standard Time**.

Avoid assuming a train will have seats available on a holiday or a popular Friday evening.

## How does seat selection work?

Depending on the channel and train, you may be able to choose seats during the booking process. Review the carriage and seat details, particularly if you are traveling in a group.

Seat selection does not guarantee that neighboring seats will be available. If your preferred train is full, compare other departures rather than assuming a standing ticket will always be offered.

Save your confirmation and be ready to show the applicable ticket or identification when requested.

## KORAIL PASS versus ordinary tickets

The **KORAIL PASS** is intended for eligible international travelers using participating KORAIL trains, including KTX. It can be attractive if you intend to make several intercity journeys during a limited period.

But it is not a universal transport pass. **SRT is excluded**, and local buses and subways are not included simply because you have an ordinary railway pass.

The Korea Tourism Organization also introduced information about **KORAIL PASS+** in a notice dated **January 22, 2026**. This is a separate all-in-one product with additional transport-card features. Do not confuse these features with the coverage of a standard KORAIL PASS.

Before buying any pass, check the current eligibility rules, number of travel days, valid operators, seat reservation procedures and cancellation conditions directly.

## A simple decision method

Write down every intercity journey you expect to make, including the direction, travel date and required train operator.

Then compare the live total cost of separate tickets with the live price of the pass you qualify for. Consider flexibility, reservation availability and coverage—not only the headline price.

If you need just one journey, an ordinary ticket may be simpler. If you have several long-distance KORAIL journeys, a pass deserves a closer look. Neither is automatically the cheapest option.

## Before boarding

Arrive with enough time to find your platform, carriage and train number. Big railway stations serve multiple train categories. Review the departure board rather than relying only on your destination name.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can international visitors book KTX online?**  
Yes. Official booking channels offer services for overseas travelers, although payment methods should be checked during purchase.

**Does the standard KORAIL PASS include SRT?**  
No. Check the official operator list and do not confuse KORAIL PASS with KORAIL PASS+ transport-card features.

**Is a KORAIL PASS always cheaper?**  
No. Compare actual fares, train coverage and your itinerary before paying.
