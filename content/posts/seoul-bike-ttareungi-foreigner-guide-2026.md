<p><em>Reviewed November 17, 2026. Check Seoul's official service for changes.</em></p>
<p>Want to explore Seoul by bicycle without owning one? Seoul Bike, or <strong>Ttareungi (따릉이)</strong>, is the city's public bike-sharing service. Stations are located across Seoul, including near subway stations, parks and residential areas. A short ride can complement public transport for visitors and international students.</p>
<p>This guide explains rental prices, foreign visitor access, QR unlocking, safe riding and correct returns.</p>
<h2>What is Seoul Bike?</h2>
<p>Seoul Bike is operated by Seoul Metropolitan Government. Riders can collect a bicycle at a participating station and return it at another. Check the official app or website for station locations and available bikes before setting off.</p>
<p>For your first ride, choose a familiar route with limited traffic. Paths near Hangang River parks are attractive options, but always follow local signs and give pedestrians space.</p>
<h2>How much does Seoul Bike cost in 2026?</h2>
<p>Seoul's official pricing page lists these <strong>one-day pass</strong> options for members and non-members:</p>
<table><thead><tr><th>Pass</th><th>Published price</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>60-minute rental option</td><td>KRW 1,000</td></tr><tr><td>120-minute rental option</td><td>KRW 2,000</td></tr><tr><td>180-minute rental option</td><td>KRW 3,000</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p>The three-hour option was introduced on <strong>November 13, 2025</strong>, according to the city.</p>
<p>Understand the difference between <strong>pass validity</strong> and each rental's time allowance. The official guidance says one-day pass validity begins with the first rental. For standard 60- and 120-minute passes, riders can return and rent again during the validity period, but each rental has a separate time limit. Extra fees apply after that limit. Verify the latest rules for the 180-minute option before purchase.</p>
<h2>How can foreigners rent a Seoul Bike?</h2>
<p>Seoul published foreign-visitor rental instructions on <strong>November 6, 2023</strong>. Its guide shows a “Foreigner” option in the Seoul Bike app. Follow these steps:</p>
<ol><li>Install the official Seoul Bike app or use the service's current instructions.</li><li>Select the foreign-visitor option and choose a rental pass.</li><li>Enter the requested email information and review the terms.</li><li>Pay using a supported method; check compatibility before traveling.</li><li>Find an available bicycle at an official rental station.</li><li>Scan its QR code or follow the relevant bicycle-number procedure.</li></ol>
<p>The city introduced an English-language assistance chatbot in November 2023. Since interfaces and payment support change, use the current app rather than relying on old screenshots.</p>
<h2>What should you check before riding?</h2>
<p>Inspect the brakes, tires, seat, handlebar and any visible damage. If a bicycle appears unsafe, choose another and report the issue using the service's official support channel.</p>
<p>Choose designated cycling routes where possible. Ride carefully at intersections, avoid using a phone while moving and slow down near pedestrians. Rain, snow, ice and poor visibility can make even familiar journeys hazardous.</p>
<h2>How do you return a Seoul Bike?</h2>
<p>Find a participating rental station, park the bicycle and secure its lock using the current instructions. Seoul specifically advises riders to <strong>check their smartphone for a return confirmation</strong>.</p>
<p>Do not assume the ride has ended just because the bicycle is parked. If confirmation does not appear, check the app and contact the service before leaving. Unresolved returns can lead to extra charges.</p>
<h2>Is Seoul Bike useful for international students?</h2>
<p>Possibly. If your accommodation and campus are close, a bicycle can help with local journeys. Compare the route, traffic, hills, weather, rental-station availability and total cost against buses and subways.</p>
<p>Try a short ride before relying on cycling for your regular commute. Travel between distant campuses may be easier by public transportation.</p>
<h2>First-time rider checklist</h2>
<ul><li>Confirm the pass price and individual rental time limit.</li><li>Check whether your payment method is supported.</li><li>Find a safe route and inspect the bicycle.</li><li>Track time to avoid extra charges.</li><li>Return at a participating station.</li><li>Wait for return confirmation before leaving.</li></ul>
<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<h3>Can foreign tourists use Seoul Bike?</h3><p>Yes. Seoul's official guidance explains a foreign-visitor rental option. Confirm the current app and payment requirements.</p>
<h3>How much is the three-hour option?</h3><p>The published price is KRW 3,000. The option began on November 13, 2025.</p>
<h3>Can I return my bicycle at a different station?</h3><p>Yes, at participating Seoul Bike stations. Always confirm the return in the app.</p>
<h2>Final advice</h2><p>Ttareungi can make short journeys around Seoul more flexible. Begin with a modest route, read the pass terms and make sure every ride ends with an official confirmation.</p>
