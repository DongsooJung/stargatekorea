The **Suneung** (수능, short for 대학수학능력시험) is South Korea's national college entrance exam. The sitting that admits students for the 2027 academic year is on **Thursday, November 19, 2026**. Scores are scheduled to be released on **Friday, December 11, 2026**.

You do not need to take the exam for it to shape your day. About **551,864** people are expected to sit it, at **1,312** test sites across **85** districts, from **8:40 a.m. to 5:45 p.m.** The city rearranges traffic and noise around them.

## What the exam actually is

Korean universities use the Suneung, together with school records and other admissions tracks, to select students. The sections are Korean, mathematics, English, Korean history, an inquiry section (social studies, science or vocational subjects) and an optional second foreign language or classical Chinese.

Korean history is mandatory. English, Korean history, and the second foreign language or classical Chinese are graded on an absolute scale. The other sections report a standard score, a percentile and a grade. The Ministry of Education set this structure, the November 19 date and the December 11 score date in its basic plan for the exam.

If you are an international student on a language course or a degree program, this is not your exam unless a Korean university has told you to take it. It still matters because campuses, hagwons and the commute change for the day.

## What changes on November 19

The Ministry of Education reported these measures to the Cabinet on October 20, 2026. They are cooperation requests and operating plans, not a promise that every shop will close.

- **Morning commute.** Public offices and companies are asked to start work **after 10:00 a.m.**, so test-takers are not stuck in the usual rush. In the Seoul capital area, subway service is increased from **6:00 a.m. to 8:10 a.m.**
- **Streets around test sites.** Vehicles are controlled from **200 meters** in front of each site. Police and local offices also run extra transport along routes students use.
- **English listening, 1:10–1:35 p.m.** Takeoffs and landings for planes and helicopters are shifted so they do not overlap this window. Military drills that make noise, including artillery fire and tank movement, pause. Outside that window, buses and trains near test sites are asked to slow down and avoid horns, and nearby events and construction sites are asked to keep noise down.
- **Weather.** From November 13, candidates can check site forecasts on [weather.go.kr](https://www.weather.go.kr).

A cafe next to a high school may be quiet. A neighborhood with no test site may feel ordinary. Check whether a school on your commute is a test site before you promise someone a 9 a.m. meeting nearby.

## If you live, study or travel here that week

**Leave earlier than usual, or later than the student rush.** The extra trains are for the 6:00–8:10 a.m. window. After that, the requested 10:00 a.m. start time is meant to thin the traffic. A trip that is easy at 11:00 can be slow at 8:00 near a high school.

**Do not expect a flight at lunchtime to leave on a normal schedule.** The listening-test window is when aircraft movements are adjusted. If you are flying on November 19, confirm the flight that morning.

**Keep noise down if you live beside a test site.** Horns, building work and outdoor events are exactly what the measures ask people to hold. Headphones are a better plan than a speaker between 1:10 and 1:35 p.m.

**Ask your university, do not assume class is cancelled.** Many campuses and hagwons change their own timetables. That decision is theirs. Look at the notice from your international office rather than a general news headline.

**This is a bad week to plan a loud group trip.** [Campus Trips](/campus-trips/) are not open for booking yet. When dates are announced, exam week is the week to skip.

## Quick checklist for November 19

- [ ] The exam runs 8:40 a.m. to 5:45 p.m.
- [ ] Offices are asked to start after 10:00 a.m. Your own employer may or may not follow that.
- [ ] Capital-area subways add trains from 6:00 to 8:10 a.m.
- [ ] Roads are controlled from 200 meters in front of test sites.
- [ ] 1:10–1:35 p.m. is the English listening window: flights shift, and nearby noise is discouraged.
- [ ] Scores are scheduled for December 11, 2026.
- [ ] Confirm your own class, flight and appointment. The national plan does not rewrite them for you.
