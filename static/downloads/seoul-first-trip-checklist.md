# Stargate Korea — First Seoul Trip Checklist

Free editorial resource · Updated November 12, 2026
https://www.stargatekorea.net/seoul-starter/

## Before departure

- [ ] Confirm your arrival and departure airport terminal.
- [ ] Save your hotel address in Korean and English, plus its phone number.
- [ ] Check check-in, late-arrival and luggage-storage arrangements.
- [ ] Check airport transport hours and the final connection to your hotel.
- [ ] Compare roaming, SIM and eSIM options; confirm device compatibility.
- [ ] Save important confirmations and contact details offline.
- [ ] Check current entry requirements with the relevant official authorities.

## Arrival day

- [ ] Get connected before leaving the airport if needed.
- [ ] Follow your chosen route to the hotel, checking current service status.
- [ ] Set up a transport card and check current top-up rules.
- [ ] Keep the first evening light: a short walk and a nearby meal.

## Each sightseeing day

- [ ] Recheck opening calendars and any required reservations.
- [ ] Group stops by neighborhood and leave room for breaks.
- [ ] Check weather and choose an indoor alternative when useful.
- [ ] Check routes and station exits for your own access needs.
- [ ] Respect residential lanes, posted hours and private entrances.

## Before purchasing an experience

- [ ] Confirm the exact date, meeting point and language.
- [ ] Compare total price, inclusions, eligibility and cancellation terms.
- [ ] Save the provider's confirmation and contact details.

## Before leaving

- [ ] Recheck airport journey, terminal and service hours.
- [ ] Allow time for check-out, luggage and airport procedures.
- [ ] Keep essentials and your travel documents accessible.
